GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is ROOMFORGE really free? +

Yes. RoomForge's planner and calculations are free to use, with no account required.

How accurate are the estimates? +

Floor area and box quantities use a transparent formula with your room dimensions, waste allowance and box coverage. Room irregularities, cutting patterns, labor, trim and subfloor conditions can change real-world requirements.

Can I use meters instead of feet? +

Yes. Switch units in the studio and the physical dimensions are preserved.

Can I save my plan? +

Your recent settings are saved locally in this browser. You can also share a configuration link or print a shopping summary.
